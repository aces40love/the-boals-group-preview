Why geography matters

A statewide label can hide very different local evidence.

Lease structures, development patterns, land utility, buyer pools, and the availability of comparable data can shift materially from one Tennessee market to the next.

Coverage is more than willingness to travel. It means approaching each assignment with the research plan and local context the property requires.
