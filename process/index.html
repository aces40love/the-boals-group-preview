Our process

A disciplined path from property question to supported conclusion.

The work changes with the property and purpose. The commitment to a defined scope, relevant evidence, careful analysis, and clear communication does not.

Connected phases. One accountable assignment.

How the work moves

No mystery between engagement and report.

A commercial appraisal is a sequence of decisions. This is the framework behind them.

  1. Discuss & define

    We begin with the assignment—not a template.

    We identify the property, client, intended users, intended use, property rights, effective date, timing, and reporting needs. If documents or requirements are missing, this is where we identify them.

    Typical outputs
    • Assignment questions
    • Conflict and competency considerations
    • Proposed scope, timing, and fee

  2. Inspect & investigate

    The evidence base is built deliberately.

    Depending on scope, the work may include property inspection, public-record and document review, market interviews, comparable research, lease and operating analysis, and broader economic or submarket context.

    Typical outputs
    • Property facts and condition
    • Relevant market evidence
    • Documented data sources and assumptions

  3. Analyze & reconcile

    Methods serve the question; they do not replace judgment.

    Applicable valuation approaches are developed using the available evidence. Results are tested for consistency, assumptions are made explicit, and the indications are reconciled into a supportable conclusion.

    Typical outputs
    • Applicable valuation approaches
    • Sensitivity and reasonableness checks
    • Reconciled conclusion

  4. Report & communicate

    The reasoning should be usable after delivery.

    The final report communicates the scope, data, analysis, assumptions, and conclusion at the level the assignment requires. Questions are handled in the context of the completed work and applicable independence obligations.

    Typical outputs
    • Assignment-specific report
    • Clear exhibits and supporting discussion
    • Post-delivery context when appropriate
