About The Boals Group

Serious about the analysis. Human in the way we work.

The Boals Group focuses on commercial real estate appraisal and valuation work across a 62-county service area in West and Middle Tennessee.

Our point of view

A good appraisal does more than state a conclusion.

It defines the assignment carefully, distinguishes fact from assumption, weighs the available market evidence, and explains the reasoning in a way the intended user can follow.

That standard shapes how we communicate from the first conversation through final delivery. The goal is not to make a complex property look simple. It is to make the analysis clear.

Working principles

What we want every assignment to feel like.

Not slogans. Practical standards for the work and the relationship around it.

01

Defined before developed

The right property interest, effective date, intended use, users, and assumptions are established before analysis.

02

Evidence over adjectives

Relevant data, property facts, and market context carry more weight than broad claims.

03

Judgment made visible

The report should show how evidence was weighed and why the conclusion follows.

04

Direct, useful communication

Questions, updates, and explanations should help move the assignment forward.
